Your first property

Add your first property to ReiSearch as a draft and learn what information is needed.

3 min read

Adding a property creates a draft that you can fill out, share, or publish later. The platform auto-fills tax record data when you enter the address, so most fields populate themselves.

Step-by-step guide

1

Open Add Property

Two ways: click "Add New Property" on the Dashboard quick action card, or click Properties in the sidebar, then click the + button or "Add New Property."

2

Enter the address

Type the street number and name. Results populate as you type. Select the correct address from the dropdown. ReiSearch pulls the tax assessor record for that address — lot size, year built, assessed value, and property characteristics. Review these values; you can edit them later.

3

Fill required fields

Property type — Single Family, Multi-Family, Condo, Townhome, or other. Price — The asking price or your estimated value. Property status — Set to "Draft" initially. You can change this later.

4

Add optional details

Recommended: beds and baths, square footage, year built, photos (the first becomes the cover image), and notes about repair estimates, seller motivation, or timelines.

5

Save as draft

Click "Save Draft." The property appears in your Properties list under the Drafts tab. You can return anytime to add more information. Drafts are only visible to you.

Draft vs. complete
A draft is a basic record visible only to you. A property is ready to share when it has a confirmed address, property type, price, at least one photo, and any deal-specific details. You don't need to finish everything in one sitting.

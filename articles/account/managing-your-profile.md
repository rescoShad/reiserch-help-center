## Help Content

Your profile is the public face of your ReiSearch account. Keep it current so people can identify you, trust your business, and reach you when a deal or connection matters.

### 1. Open Profile Settings

Go to Settings > Profile. This is where you update your public-facing information, photos, bio, social links, and company details.

### 2. Update Your Photos

- Cover photo — a banner image that sets the tone for your profile.
- Profile picture — a headshot or logo that appears across ReiSearch.

Use clear, professional images so your profile looks complete at a glance.

[SCREENSHOT: Profile settings page with cover photo and profile picture upload areas]

### 3. Edit Your About Me Details

Keep these fields accurate:

- Name — use the name you want displayed on ReiSearch.
- Phone number — the number people should use to reach you.
- Email — make sure the address is current.
- Location — add the markets or cities you work in.
- About Me — explain what you do and what kinds of deals you focus on.

### 4. Add Social Links

If you use professional social profiles, add them here:

- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube

These links help others verify you and learn more about your business.

### 5. Set Your Signature and Company Info

If you generate contracts or presentations in ReiSearch, set your digital signature and company details so documents are ready to send without extra cleanup.

### 6. Save Your Changes

Click Save Changes when you’re done. If you leave the page without saving, you may lose unsaved edits.

### Best Practices

- Use a consistent name and photo across platforms.
- Keep your phone and email current.
- Fill out the bio with enough detail that people understand your role.
- Update the profile any time your business, market, or contact info changes.
